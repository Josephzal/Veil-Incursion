Encounter: Rummaging the Scrap (Digging in Trash)
Text: "A rusted municipal dumpster overflows with old, damp cardboard. Beneath the trash, a faint, rhythmic scratching sound scratches at the plastic lining, accompanied by a cold, copper stench that smells exactly like fresh blood."
Option A (Class Blueprint Extraction): Use your signature weapon to peel back the heavy iron lid safely without exposing your vitals. Requires a class-locked action check (triggers tap in time mini game):
Aegis: Use the flat of your weapon to leverage the lid up while maintaining a defensive posture.
Riftshot: Wedge the heavy cylinder of your revolver into the gap to flip it open from a safe distance.
Envoy: Guide your pendulum to slide the locking bar loose using an invisible tether of pressure.
Result: Success opens the dumpster cleanly, rewarding you with a single-use Curdling Elixir run-item (+15% Shield Integrity instantly). Failure snaps the heavy lid down on your hands, causing a -10 Health penalty and spilling foul, localized corruption onto your gear.
Option B (The Blind Grab): Thrust your arms directly into the refuse to yank out whatever is making the noise. You bypass the skill check completely but plunge your hand straight into a jagged, broken mirror used in an old ritual. You suffer a permanent -5% Max Stamina penalty for the rest of the run due to deep tendon strain, but pull out a Shattered Focus Lens run-item (guarantees a Critical Strike on your next combat turn).
